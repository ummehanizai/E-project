Technology & Craft

Every Alberto watch combines precision, craftsmanship and innovation to deliver exceptional performance and timeless style.

⚙

Automatic Movement

Uses the motion of the wearer's wrist to wind a mechanical spring. No battery is needed for the movement.

◷

Quartz Precision

A battery powers a quartz crystal oscillator to keep time accurately with minimal maintenance.

⌁

Smart Technology

Digital features can include activity tracking, notifications, timers and phone connectivity.
